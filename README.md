DIGITAL BUSINESS CARD — TEMPLATE

1. Open index.html and replace:
   YOUR NAME, COMPANY, phone, WhatsApp, Telegram, email and links.

2. Edit contact.vcf with the same contact details.

3. Upload index.html and contact.vcf to a free static hosting service such as GitHub Pages.

4. After you have your final public page URL, generate the final QR code from that URL.
   The included qr-placeholder.png is only a placeholder.

Recommended:
- Keep the QR pointed to the page URL, not directly to the contact file.
- Then you can change your links later without reprinting the QR.
